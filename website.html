Skin journeys

Fifteen minutes to talk. Then a plan you can hold.

Your consultation is free, and there is no pressure to go ahead with anything. If we do, you leave with a written skin journey: three to six months, mapped fortnightly, with your homecare and your budget, and we follow it up with you rather than hoping you remember.

01

We talk

Your history, your goals, what you have tried. A proper look at your skin, and photos taken the same way every time so you can see the change.

02

We plan

A written journey: what, in what order, how often. SQT, microneedling, peels, LED, IPL, cosmetic medicine where it fits. Sent to you the same day.

03

We follow up

A text the next day, a little education about your pathway, and a reminder when your next fortnight comes around. No chasing.

04

We keep going

Your next appointment is booked before you leave. Your three products are reordered before you run out. Your progress photos tell the story.

Do I need to know what treatment I want?

No. Tell us what you are noticing. The consultation is free and works out the plan, and we explain every option before anything is booked.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Around fifteen minutes, no pressure to go ahead. If we plan a journey together, your first SQT at the intro price includes it.

Can I see results before I book?

Skin results are shared with consent on this site. Results for prescription-only cosmetic treatments are shared after your consultation, in line with Australian advertising rules.
